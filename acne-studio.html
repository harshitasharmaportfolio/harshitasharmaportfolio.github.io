Acne Studios

this project is a creative campaign concept developed from an analysis of Acne Studios FW25, where I translated the collection’s bold tailoring, layered textures, and urban-natural contrasts into a visual narrative. Through moodboarding, casting, styling, location planning, and creative direction, I built a campaign world that extends the runway collection into a cohesive editorial story.

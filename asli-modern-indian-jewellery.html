Asli (Modern Indian Jewellery)

ART DIRECTION PROJECT

Asli (Modern Indian Jewellery)

This is a self-initiated art direction project based on a hypothetical brand called Asli. The idea came from something I keep noticing on social media, i.e., artisans and their work are often used without proper credit or context. With Asli, I wanted to explore how a brand can still feel modern and relevant, but also be honest about where things come from. The focus is on giving visibility and credit to the actual artisans, while building a strong visual identity around it. It is an attempt to rethink branding in a way that feels both contemporary and responsible.

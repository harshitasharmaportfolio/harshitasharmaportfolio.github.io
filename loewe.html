Loewe – Collaborative Campaign Study

This project is a small collaboration where my friend developed a strategic case study around Loewe’s universe, and I translated that research into an experimental campaign image shot in my own space. I handled all photography, direction and layout, working with an off‑kilter perspective, hard flash and a single yellow umbrella as a sculptural prop to echo Loewe’s playful, object‑driven styling. The result is a one‑look visual that feels slightly surreal and intimate, sitting somewhere between a fashion campaign and a personal visual diary.

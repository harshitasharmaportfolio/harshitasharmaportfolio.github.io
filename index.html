A little more about me.

I grew up in a small town in Rajasthan, India. The first eighteen years of my life were spent in the same school, with the same faces, on the same streets. Looking back, all that time spent watching and noticing things was quietly shaping how I see the world.

NIFT is where I found photography. I fell in love with the whole process, from the first idea to the final image. It was the first time I felt like I could say something honest without needing words. I have always had a lot going on inside, and photography gave that a place to go.

During COVID, I had no studio and no equipment. So I made my own lights and used things from around the house to create photographs. I did not stop. That is just how I am with things I care about.

After NIFT, I received offer letters from schools I had been dreaming about for a long time — RCA, Speos, Parsons, Leeds. I ended up in Grenoble instead, and honestly, it turned out to be one of the best things that happened to me. That city felt like home in a way I had not expected. I am in Paris now for my second year, still figuring things out.

What I keep coming back to is that visuals, books, stories, and concepts all connect for me. They feed into each other and build into something. Language becomes image. Image becomes the identity of a brand. That is the loop I live in, and it is why art direction feels like the right space for me. It is where an image can carry a whole story.

There is more to me than I usually put out there. It does not always come out in words, but it finds its way into everything I make.

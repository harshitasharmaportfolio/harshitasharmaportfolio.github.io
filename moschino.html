BRAND & RETAIL STUDY

Moschino – Brand & Retail Study

A collaborative deep dive into Moschino’s personality, from its playful brand codes (teddy bears, hearts, trompe‑l’œil) to how that attitude comes alive in stores across Milan, Paris, New York, Tokyo and Beijing. We explored how each space reflects the spirit of its city while staying true to Moschino’s irreverent, theatrical luxury.

Credits

Research, analysis, writing and presentation: Parthvi Thakkar, Abbigail Karsten, Harshita Sharma, Karan Verma.
